Delete your account

Last updated: July 14, 2026

You can permanently delete your FirstPass account and all of your study data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Fastest way — in the app. Open Settings → Account → Delete, type DELETE to confirm, and your account is erased immediately. No email, no waiting.

If you can’t open the app

Email brandon.postlethwait@yahoo.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my FirstPass account”.

The request must come from the account’s own email address — that’s how I confirm it’s really you. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your name, if you provided one
  • Every practice exam and quiz attempt, with scores and dates
  • Lessons completed and knowledge-check scores
  • Flashcard mastery, study plan, and app settings
  • Your subscription status record

Deletion is permanent and immediate — there is no grace period and no way to recover the data afterwards. Nothing is retained.

Important: cancel your subscription first

If you subscribe to Pro, deleting your account does not cancel that subscription. Apple bills it, not me, and I have no ability to cancel it on your behalf.

Cancel it first on your iPhone or iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → FirstPass → Cancel Subscription. If you delete your account without cancelling, Apple will keep charging you.
